    How to use

    Wire: drag a core end onto a screw or push-in terminal, or tap the core end and then tap the terminal. Drag it off again to take it out. A core is one piece of copper from end to end, so it is the same conductor at both ends.
    Zoom: mouse wheel, the + / − buttons, or drag the background to move around.
    Polarity tester (power ON): tap two points; green = red probe on the + side.
    Multimeter Ω (power OFF): ring out cores, or measure the Pt100.
    mA clamp (power ON): tap any core to read the loop current in it.

    Values used: transmitter output 4-20 mA, saturation and alarm levels, the card's raw values (0 at 4 mA, 27648 at 20 mA, 32511 top of the overshoot range at 22.81 mA, -4864 at 1.185 mA, 32767 overflow, -32768 underflow), the terminal numbers and the wiring rules all come from the device manuals. Where a manual gives no number, the simulator shows none. Your own manuals and site standards come first.
